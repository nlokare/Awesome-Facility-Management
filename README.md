# Awesome-Facility-Management

## Top Facility Management Software Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Facilities Operations, Space Management, Maintenance, Asset Management & Workplace Management*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Facility Management (FM)**. These tools help organizations manage buildings, facilities, workspaces, assets, maintenance, work orders, space planning, leases, energy, contractors, workplace experience, and facility-related operations.

**Examples** include Archibus, FM:Systems, OfficeSpace, Spacewell, IBM TRIRIGA, Planon, ServiceChannel, Facilio, Eptura, and UpKeep.

**Open-source emphasis**: There are considerably fewer complete open-source equivalents to enterprise IWMS and facility-management suites. Therefore, this section is expanded with open-source **CAFM, CMMS, asset-management, work-order, field-service, building-management, IoT, GIS, BIM, workplace, and maintenance** projects that can be combined into a self-hosted facility-management stack. In particular, **openMAINT** is a direct open-source Property & Facility Management solution, while ERPNext and related Frappe applications provide broader open-source asset, maintenance, service, and operations capabilities. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or project repositories.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[openMAINT](https://www.openmaint.org/)**  
  One of the strongest direct open-source alternatives in this category. openMAINT is specifically designed for **Property & Facility Management**, covering buildings, infrastructure, technical systems, furniture, assets, maintenance, logistics, economics, energy, GIS, and BIM-related information. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

- **[CMDBuild](https://github.com/CMDBuild/CMDBuild)**  
  Open-source platform for configuring and managing custom asset and infrastructure information systems. It forms the underlying technology for openMAINT and can be adapted to facility and asset-management workflows. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Broad open-source ERP platform with asset management, maintenance, projects, inventory, procurement, accounting, and service capabilities. Its asset-management functionality can serve as the operational foundation of a custom facility-management system. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

- **[Frappe Framework](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)**  
  Open-source full-stack framework underlying ERPNext. Useful for building custom facility-management applications, workflows, dashboards, REST APIs, asset registers, maintenance systems, and approval processes. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

- **[One-FM](https://github.com/ONE-F-M/One-FM)**  
  Open-source facilities-management application built on ERPNext, designed around commercial automation and integrated facility/security-management operations. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

- **[Beveren Field Service Management](https://github.com/Beveren-Software-Inc/Field_Service_Management)**  
  Open-source ERPNext field-service application for service requests, job scheduling, technician assignment, on-site work, spare parts, and invoicing. It can serve as a building-maintenance and field-service component. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  
  Open-source project-management platform useful for facility construction projects, renovations, capital projects, maintenance programs, and facility improvement initiatives.

- **[ERPNext Assets](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  ERPNext's asset-management functionality supports asset lifecycle management from acquisition through disposal, providing a useful foundation for facilities and equipment inventories. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**  
  Open-source asset-management platform useful for tracking equipment, devices, ownership, locations, maintenance history, and asset assignments across facilities.

- **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)**  
  Open-source IT and asset-management platform with inventory, service management, locations, contracts, tickets, and asset lifecycle capabilities that can be extended to facility-related assets.

- **[ITop](https://github.com/Combodo/iTop)**  
  Open-source configuration and asset-management platform useful for modeling infrastructure, relationships, locations, equipment, and operational services.

- **[Ralph](https://github.com/allegro/ralph)**  
  Open-source asset-management and data-center infrastructure management platform that can be adapted for physical equipment and facility asset inventories.

- **[NetBox](https://github.com/netbox-community/netbox)**  
  Open-source infrastructure resource-management platform useful for documenting locations, racks, equipment, physical infrastructure, and relationships.

- **[Home Assistant](https://github.com/home-assistant/core)**  
  Open-source home/building automation platform with extensive integrations for sensors, HVAC, lighting, energy, occupancy, and environmental monitoring. It can act as an IoT layer in smaller facility deployments.

- **[OpenRemote](https://github.com/openremote/openremote)**  
  Open-source IoT and building-management platform for connecting devices, sensors, energy systems, automation rules, and dashboards.

- **[ThingsBoard](https://github.com/thingsboard/thingsboard)**  
  Open-source IoT platform supporting device management, telemetry, dashboards, alarms, rule engines, and facility/building sensor integrations.

- **[Mainflux](https://github.com/mainflux/mainflux)**  
  Open-source IoT platform for device connectivity, messaging, data collection, and management of connected physical infrastructure.

- **[Eclipse Ditto](https://github.com/eclipse-ditto/ditto)**  
  Open-source IoT framework for creating digital twins of physical devices and assets, useful for smart-building and facility-management architectures.

- **[Eclipse IoT](https://github.com/eclipse-iot)**  
  Collection of open-source IoT technologies useful for connecting facility sensors, building systems, devices, and operational platforms.

- **[Node-RED](https://github.com/node-red/node-red)**  
  Open-source flow-based programming platform useful for integrating building sensors, BACnet gateways, APIs, automation systems, and facility-management workflows.

- **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)**  
  Open-source observability and visualization platform useful for facility dashboards covering energy, occupancy, equipment health, environmental conditions, and maintenance KPIs.

- **[InfluxDB](https://github.com/influxdata/influxdb)**  
  Time-series database useful for storing HVAC, energy, occupancy, temperature, humidity, equipment, and IoT telemetry.

- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**  
  Open-source analytics and dashboarding platform useful for facility KPIs, maintenance reporting, occupancy analysis, energy consumption, and operational reporting.

- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  
  Open-source business-intelligence platform that can provide self-service reporting and dashboards over facility and asset-management databases.

- **[PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)**  
  Open-source analytics platform that can be adapted for workplace-service usage analytics and employee interaction data.

- **[QGIS](https://github.com/qgis/QGIS)**  
  Open-source GIS platform useful for mapping facilities, campuses, buildings, utilities, infrastructure, assets, and geographic maintenance activities.

- **[FreeCAD](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD)**  
  Open-source parametric CAD platform that can support building, equipment, infrastructure, and facility-engineering workflows.

- **[BlenderBIM](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell)**  
  Open-source BIM ecosystem built around IFC/OpenBIM technologies, useful for building models, facility information, spatial data, and BIM-to-FM workflows.

- **[IfcOpenShell](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell)**  
  Open-source software library for working with IFC building models and OpenBIM data, providing an important interoperability layer for BIM-enabled facility management.

- **[Bonsai](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell)**  
  OpenBIM toolkit for Blender that can help connect building models with facility information and operational workflows.

- **[FreeCAD BIM](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD)**  
  Open-source CAD/BIM environment that can contribute building geometry and engineering information to facility-management workflows.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Open-Source CMMS Platforms** for work orders, preventive maintenance, inspections, spare parts, and technician workflows.
- **Open-Source CAFM Platforms** for space management, asset inventories, building information, and facility operations.
- **Asset Management Systems** for equipment lifecycle, ownership, location, depreciation, and maintenance history.
- **Field Service Management Systems** for dispatching technicians, scheduling jobs, mobile work, and service reporting.
- **Building Automation Platforms** for HVAC, lighting, access control, environmental monitoring, and automation.
- **IoT Platforms** for collecting telemetry from facility sensors and connected building systems.
- **Energy Monitoring Platforms** for electricity, gas, water, HVAC, and sustainability data.
- **Digital Twin Platforms** for representing buildings, equipment, spaces, and connected assets.
- **GIS Platforms** for campus, property, infrastructure, and geographically distributed facility management.
- **OpenBIM / IFC Tools** for connecting BIM models to CAFM/IWMS systems.
- **Time-Series Databases** for high-frequency HVAC, energy, occupancy, and equipment telemetry.
- **Open-Source BI Tools** for facility dashboards, maintenance KPIs, energy analytics, and executive reporting.
- **Workflow Automation Tools** for automating work requests, approvals, preventive-maintenance schedules, and vendor processes.
- **Document Management Platforms** for storing equipment manuals, floor plans, compliance records, inspection documents, and facility documentation.
- **Digital Twin Frameworks** for creating real-time representations of buildings and physical assets.

**Frameworks for building custom facility-management platforms**: Combine **openMAINT/CMDBuild** for the core property and facility-management data model, **ERPNext/Frappe** for business operations and asset workflows, **OpenRemote/ThingsBoard/Eclipse Ditto** for IoT and digital twins, **QGIS + IfcOpenShell** for GIS/BIM, and **InfluxDB + Grafana** for real-time facility telemetry and dashboards. This combination can provide a highly customizable self-hosted alternative to portions of traditional CAFM/IWMS platforms. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow existing format).
3. Include: name, link, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it's SaaS or open-source.
4. Prioritize genuinely open-source projects with an accessible repository or clearly documented open license.
5. Submit PR with a short explanation.

Star the repo if you find it useful!

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Facility-management software spans CAFM, CMMS, IWMS, workplace management, asset management, building automation, energy management, and field service.
- Most open-source projects listed here are **building blocks rather than complete enterprise IWMS platforms**.
- Open-source licensing varies; verify the current license before using a project commercially.
- Building-management and IoT deployments may require specialized hardware, networking, safety controls, and professional engineering.
- BIM/IFC, GIS, IoT, and building-automation integrations may require additional configuration and domain-specific expertise.
- SaaS capabilities, product names, ownership, pricing, and licensing models can change over time.
